# Synthetic Truck Dispatch SOP

> Synthetic demonstration document. This is not an operational MRPL procedure.

## 1. Fleet sizing

Review expected daily dispatch demand, loading-slot capacity, truck turnaround time, queue time, and safety constraints before changing fleet capacity.

## 2. Appointment scheduling

Use truck appointment slots and daily dispatch forecasts to reduce queueing, idle capacity, and avoidable turnaround delays.

## 3. Review metrics

Track truck utilization, queue time, turnaround time, late dispatches, loading-bay utilization, and safety events during the pilot period.

## 4. Human approval

Changes to contracted fleet capacity require documented operational review and human approval. AI-generated analysis must be treated as a draft.
